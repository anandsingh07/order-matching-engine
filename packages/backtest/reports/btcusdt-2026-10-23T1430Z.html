How this was simulated

  • The historical trades are taker flow. Each is replayed as an IOC order, capped at its real size, into the real matching engine (@ome/core), which holds only the strategy's resting quotes.
  • Queue model "through" (conservative): a quote fills only when the market trades strictly through its price. The tape has no depth, so queue position is unknown, and this assumes the strategy was last in the queue. Running both models shows how much a result depends on queue position; they are models of it, not exact bounds.
  • A print also proves an order on its far side: a buyer-initiated trade at a price lifted an ask resting there. A quote of ours at or through that price could not have rested beside it, so in both queue models the print fills it at its own price, capped by the print's size.
  • The rest of the market's touch comes from the tape: a buyer-initiated trade printed at the ask, a seller-initiated one at the bid, and the spread is one tick.
  • Quotes are plain limit orders, as the live bots send them (the exchange has no post-only type). A quote that crosses the touch when it arrives takes liquidity at once: it fills in full as a taker at the touch plus the slippage, never past its limit, and pays the taker fee. It is assumed to find its whole size at the touch the tape implies; live, had that level just emptied, it would rest instead. The quotes table counts these.
  • Every action takes effect 50 ms after the decision. Market data and fills reach the strategy at once. At equal timestamps the tape comes before the strategy's actions.
  • Market orders fill at the touch plus 1 bps slippage. Fees: maker 0 bps, taker 0 bps of notional.
  • The strategy's trades do not move later historical prices (no market impact).
  • Sharpe ratio: mean over standard deviation of 1-minute equity changes, times the square root of 525,600 (minutes in a year). A run this short says little about it.
  • Markouts: the last trade price 1 s, 10 s, 60 s after each fill, against the fill's price, in the fill's favour, in bps and weighted by quantity. Negative means the fills were followed by moves against them (adverse selection). They are computed after the run; no strategy sees them.
  • Where the quotes sat: each limit order's distance from the last trade price when it was decided. Quotes placed within 0.5 bps of it count as at the touch; their share of the maker fill quantity and their markouts show where the fills and the adverse selection came from.
  • "After closing" closes the final position with a market order at the end.
